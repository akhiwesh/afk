# Patient Zero Outreach — First Paying Diagnostic

**Goal:** One founder-approved, paid Revenue Constraint Diagnostic (₹49,000) delivered end-to-end.  
**Why Patient Zero matters:** Validates offer, runbook, and instrument with a real business before scaling outreach.  
**Companion docs:** [Scope of Work](./SCOPE_OF_WORK.md) · [Delivery Runbook](./DELIVERY_RUNBOOK.md) · Offer page `{BASE_URL}/#offer`

---

## 1. Who Patient Zero is

Not “anyone who might pay.” One person who matches **all** of these:

| Criterion | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| B2B services founder (agency, consultancy, studio) | Matches instrument design |
| ~₹5L–₹25L/mo revenue, repeatable offer | Enough signal, still founder-led |
| You have **warm access** (met them, mutual, or they’ve seen your work) | First sale is trust, not cold scale |
| Willing to export **invoices or CRM** (not audit-only) | Proves evidence path |
| Can commit **2 weeks + Day-90 checkpoint** | Full flywheel test |
| Will give **honest feedback** on delivery | Improves runbook before case #2 |

**Disqualify** even for Patient Zero: wants you to run outbound, pre-revenue, or “just tell me the answer in a DM.”

## 2. Founding-client trade (optional, recommended)

Patient Zero is not a discount for desperation — it’s a **structured exchange**:

| You give | They give |
|----------|-----------|
| **₹39,000** founding rate (₹10k off list) | Full fee paid before kickoff |
| Same deliverables as SOW §2 | Complete audit + at least one evidence upload |
| Extra 20-min feedback call after checkpoint | Written feedback (5 questions, §8) |
| First right to refuse public case study | Opt-in only: anonymized case or named quote |

If they won’t do evidence uploads, keep list price (₹49,000) or pass — audit-only doesn’t validate the product.

## 3. Outreach order (do in sequence)

1. **List 10 names** — warm only for Patient Zero (no spray-and-pray).  
2. **Pick 3** — strongest fit + fastest reply likelihood.  
3. **Send Template A or B** — one channel per person (don’t multi-ping same day).  
4. **Offer demo link** — `{BASE_URL}/#offer` → “Try demo business” or you screen-share demo on a 15-min call.  
5. **Fit call** → SOW → payment → runbook.  

**Do not** mention “Patient Zero” or “we’re testing” in client-facing copy. Say **founding diagnostic slot** if you mention the reduced rate.

## 11. Objection handling (copy-paste snippets)

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| “Can you just audit my business quickly for free?” | “The demo business is the free version — full synthetic diagnosis at {BASE_URL}/#offer. Paid work is founder-led interpretation of *your* evidence; that’s the SOW.” |
| “I already know it’s leads.” | “That’s exactly what we test. Most founders anchor on demand; the engine often surfaces conversion or pricing when invoices are uploaded. Worth validating before spending on ads.” |
| “₹39k/49k is a lot.” | “Comparable exec diagnosis from a consultant is often ₹50k+ without a repeatable instrument or Day-90 checkpoint. Founding slot is ₹39k with feedback — one slot only.” |
| “I don’t have clean exports.” | “Messy CSV is fine. If you have Zoho/FreshBooks/Excel, we can do a 10-min screen-share on export. Without files, diagnosis stays low-confidence — we’ll say so explicitly.” |
| “Will revenue go up?” | “We don’t guarantee uplift. We identify the primary constraint, cite evidence, and track dimension movement at Day 90. Execution is yours.” |
| “Is this AI?” | “Deterministic rules + your data. AI doesn’t invent your close rate. I walk you through findings on the diagnosis call.” |

## 14. Five feedback questions (post-checkpoint)

1. What was the **most surprising** finding vs what you believed before?

3. What was **missing** from the diagnosis or PDF?  
4. Would you pay **₹49,000** for this again, or refer a peer? Why / why not?  
5. May we use **anonymized** learnings to improve AFK? Named quote on the site? (Y / anonymized only / no)

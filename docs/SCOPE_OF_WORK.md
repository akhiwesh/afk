## 1. Purpose

Client engages AFK to identify where revenue is **actually constrained** in Client’s service business — using structured audit answers and uploaded business evidence, not generic consulting advice.

AFK delivers **clarity and a prioritized treatment protocol**. AFK does **not** implement sales, marketing, pricing, or operations on Client’s behalf.

---

## 2. Services included

AFK will provide a **concierge Revenue Constraint Diagnostic** consisting of:

| # | Deliverable | Description |
|---|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Fit & kickoff** | 30-minute call to confirm scope, evidence availability, and timeline |
| 2 | **Platform access** | Access to the AFK Revenue Diagnostic instrument for the engagement period |
| 3 | **Revenue Audit guidance** | Structured audit across demand, qualification, conversion, pricing, retention, and founder dependency |
| 4 | **Evidence review** | Review of Client-uploaded exports (e.g. invoices, CRM/leads, transactions) where provided |
| 5 | **Executive diagnosis session** | ~60-minute live walkthrough of findings, confidence levels, and primary constraint |
| 6 | **Executive diagnosis PDF** | Shareable memo with health score, risk level, cited findings, and method notes |
| 7 | **90-day treatment protocol** | Constraint-specific first actions with in-platform outcome tracking (protocol AFK-OUT-0001) |
| 8 | **Checkpoint diagnosis** | One follow-up diagnosis review ~90 days after treatment start, focused on **dimension movement** — not revenue guarantees |

## 3. What is not included

The following are **explicitly out of scope** unless agreed in a separate written SOW:

- CRM setup, automation, outbound campaigns, or ad management  
- Done-for-you sales calls, lead generation, or closing on Client’s behalf  
- Bookkeeping, tax, legal, or statutory compliance work  
- Guaranteed revenue, profit, or close-rate outcomes  
- Industry benchmark rankings or percentile claims (not offered until AFK’s calibration dataset meets its public threshold)  
- Ongoing platform subscription beyond the engagement access window described in §5  
- Additional checkpoint sessions beyond the one included at ~90 days

## 4. Client responsibilities

Client agrees to:

1. **Participation** — Make the founder or revenue owner available for the fit call, diagnosis session, and checkpoint.  
2. **Revenue Audit** — Complete the in-platform Revenue Audit honestly and in full (target: within 5 business days of kickoff).  
3. **Evidence** — Upload available business exports in CSV or Excel format (target: within 7 business days of kickoff). Minimum useful exports include **any one of**: invoices, CRM/lead export, or transaction data.  
4. **Accuracy** — Provide truthful answers and authentic exports. AFK may pause or terminate the engagement if data appears fabricated.  
5. **Execution** — Implement the 90-day protocol using Client’s own team; AFK advises, Client executes.  
6. **Timeliness** — Respond to scheduling and data requests within 3 business days. Delays may shift session dates; AFK is not responsible for timeline overruns caused by Client delay beyond 14 days total pause.  

**If Client provides audit answers only (no uploads):** AFK will still deliver a diagnosis, but confidence per dimension may remain limited. AFK will state this explicitly in the session and PDF.

## 8. Confidentiality & data

1. **Client data** — Audit answers, uploads, and diagnosis outputs belong to Client. AFK uses them only to deliver this engagement.  
2. **AFK confidentiality** — AFK will not share Client’s raw exports or diagnosis with third parties without Client’s written consent.  
3. **Anonymized learning** — AFK may use **de-identified** constraint patterns to improve its instrument only with Client’s **opt-in** (separate yes/no at kickoff). Default is **no**.  
4. **Platform security** — Data is stored on AFK’s systems; Client is responsible for safeguarding login credentials.  
5. **No public marketing use** — AFK will not name Client as a case study without written permission.

## 9. Limitations & acknowledgements

Client understands and agrees:

1. **Not financial or legal advice.** The diagnostic is operational revenue clarity based on Client-supplied information. It is not investment, tax, or legal advice.  
2. **No outcome guarantee.** AFK does not guarantee revenue growth, lead volume, close rates, or profit. Opportunity figures, where shown, are **directional ranges** with formulas — not forecasts.  
3. **Evidence-bound.** Where evidence is missing, AFK will report *insufficient evidence* rather than infer numbers.  
4. **Single primary constraint.** The engagement prioritizes one primary revenue constraint by design. Secondary issues may be noted but are not the focus of the 90-day protocol.  
5. **Human + instrument.** Findings are produced by AFK’s deterministic diagnostic engine and interpreted by an AFK founder on the diagnosis call.  
6. **Treatment tracking** measures **dimension score movement** on re-diagnosis — not Client’s self-reported revenue unless supported by new evidence.
